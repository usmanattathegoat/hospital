How it works

What happens after you book

  1. 01

    We confirm your slot

    A care coordinator reviews your request and confirms by phone or email within 2 business hours.

  2. 02

    Prepare for your visit

    Bring a photo ID, insurance card and any previous medical records or current medications list.

  3. 03

    Meet your specialist

    Arrive 15 minutes early. Results and follow-ups will be available in your patient portal.
